    How the illustration relates to evidence

    Mature ribosomal structures use resolved polymer chains from PDB 4UG0, excluding experimental tRNA, ligands, water and ions. Pre-ribosomal particles use the mature structure as a visual reference, with schematic processing tails and assembly factors. They are not atomic models of each intermediate or molecular dynamics trajectories.

    The illustration highlights one pathway at a time. In cells, rRNA transcription, processing, protein synthesis and assembly overlap extensively. Processing and assembly are coupled. The 47S → 45S → subunit precursor sequence is a simplified overview, not the only human pre-rRNA cleavage pathway.

    Pol III transcribes 5S rRNA from separate genes; it enters the nuclear 5S RNP assembly pathway. NXF1–NXT1 represents mRNA export, while NLS / importin represents ribosomal protein import. Pre-subunit export involves XPO1 / CRM1 and other factors; pre-60S uses adaptors such as NMD3. The nuclear envelope has inner and outer membranes, each a lipid bilayer, separated by a perinuclear space. Its transparent surface rotates slowly to reveal nuclear events. Pores depict an eightfold scaffold, central channel, cytoplasmic filaments and nuclear basket. These are enlarged schematics, not atomic pore models. Assembly and transport factors are symbolic.

    Cytoplasmic 40S maturation depicts NOB1-mediated processing of the 18S-E end and factor recycling. The 60S pathway depicts SBDS / EFL1-mediated eIF6 release. Mature subunits join during translation initiation and can be recycled after termination. Replay represents a new biogenesis event, not mature ribosomes returning to the nucleolus. The nucleolus has no surrounding lipid membrane.
